## Decisions made

- The scenes are full painted strips (sky included), 12 px tall, tiled every
  48 px. @danielpg95 chose each concept: dusk pines with leaves, sunny shore
  with bubbles, midday dunes with sand, crystal cave with glints.
- The forest was redrawn twice: first as a denser three-layer forest, then
  thinned. The grotto lost its tunnel because tiling repeats any landmark (two
  tunnels at 100 columns); soft hollows and cracks replaced it.

## Problems and findings

- **Tiling repeats landmarks.** Any one-of-a-kind feature shows once per 48
  columns. Scenes should use features that read fine when repeated.
- **No preview for particles.** The `preview` tools only show the art. The
  animated GIF came from a scratch script that imports the mod's
  `render/idle-scene.ts` through the helper's `ts-resolve.mjs`. The script isn't
  in the repo.
