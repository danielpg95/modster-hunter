## Decisions made

- Concept: a jack-in-the-box named Jesterbox, desktop art only, `contain` style, crank turns on each rock (@danielpg95).
- All four suits glow, so they share one exact colour (@danielpg95).
- The art comes from references, not drawn in code: the first code-drawn box was dropped in favour of a reference (@danielpg95).
- A jester pops out on a catch, as a nod to Gigglet, instead of a latched lid; it doesn't pop out on a miss (@danielpg95).

## Problems and findings

- The second reference draws the box at a different angle and proportions, so the jester couldn't be grafted onto the approved box. The caught frame is the whole second reference, with its box about 8% smaller and its feet aligned with the other frames.
- The crank sits at the sheet's right edge, so the knob can only swing about 3 px sideways inside 76 px; left and right crank frames read closer together than up and down.
- A 76×96 catcher sheet still fits every built-in Modster (`check-content`).
- The cut's cream was shared by the suits and the jester's face; only the suit pixels were set to `#eacb87`, so the face doesn't glow.
