- Hollowmask's "Modster detail" is a leaf mask plus tail rings in the same leaf green. A glinting stash and twig antlers were offered and not chosen.
- The musketeer mouse moved from the common slot to the rare one, at @danielpg95's request. Bramble Lynx was dropped.

## Problems and findings

- The automatic outline only paints onto empty pixels, so overlapping parts (an ear on a log, an arm on a cape) blend together. These need a manual outline against the other material; Hollowmask's ears and tail, and Musketail's arm, do this.
- At this size, faces fail fast on low contrast. What worked: flat face tones, eyes that contrast with what's around them (ice blue on a dark mask), and no ellipse shading near a muzzle (it reads as a beak).
