## Done

- **Desktop backgrounds:**
  - `desktopIdleScene` builds a desktop scene from a `desktopBackground`.
  - `sceneSvg` embeds the sheet once and tiles it at a quarter of its pixels
    (`DESKTOP.sheetPixels`), so 48 sheet px take today's 12-px strip.
  - Its frames step at `backgroundFps` while Claude works, and it holds frame
    0 between turns. It's drawn as an image.
  - The band and the Biomes tab use it.
- **Particle art (decision 0046, new):**
  - A biome may ship `desktopParticles`, a sheet of 1–8 frames of at most
    16×16 and 4 KiB. It needs `particles`.
  - Each particle is a window on the shared sheet, centred where its square
    would be, tumbling at 8 fps from its own frame.
  - It's in the format, validator, loader, `CONTENT_FORMAT.md`, and the
    sprite tool's new `--desktop particles`.
- **Full width:**
  - The strip stopped short of the band in the Code tab: `columnPx` (8) is
    only an estimate, and a column is closer to 8.8 there.
  - It's now drawn 1.5× wider (`DESKTOP.sceneOvershoot`) with
    `preserveAspectRatio="xMinYMin slice"`, and the `Svg` gets only its
    height, so the box stops at the slot and the art is cropped there.
- **Throw flash:** a resumed desktop throw started at the last tick's time, up
  to a tick behind, so a redraw just after the Modster went inside showed it
  for an instant. Redraws now start a tick ahead, rounded up.

## Decisions made

- Decision 0046: on desktop, a biome may draw its particles with its own art
  (Accepted by @danielpg95).
- Things @danielpg95 settled while looking at the art:
  - Moving the canopy looked uncanny, so only low foliage sways.
  - Leaves at 12×12 were too big, so they're 8×8.
  - The leaves are canopy green, not bright green.
- The idle line under the scene reads too much like a terminal. @danielpg95
  agreed on a name tag in the scene with a "listening" dot, as its own task
  (P5-54) after this PR.

## Problems and findings

- `DESKTOP.columnPx` (8) is short of the Code tab's real column width (about
  8.8). Anything sized from it should overshoot and crop, as the scene now
  does.
- Particles are SMIL in an image-mode Svg too: they restart when the band
  redraws, but the band only redraws the scene when a turn starts or ends.
