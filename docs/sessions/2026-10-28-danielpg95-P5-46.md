## Done

- **Renderer:** `plugin/hooks/render/desktop-sheet-svg.ts` is the pure
  `desktopSheetSvg`. It draws a desktop sheet as one image-mode Svg:
  - the PNG is embedded as-is, behind a one-frame window;
  - SMIL steps its `x` at the sheet's `fps`;
  - it's pixelated;
  - a silhouette is the same sheet through an `feFlood`/`feComposite` gray
    filter, held on frame 0.

  Render tests are in `plugin/tests/render/desktop-sheet-svg.test.ts`.
- **Scales:** `DESKTOP.sheetPaneScale` (1), `sheetCollectionScale` (0.75) and
  `sheetBandScale` (0.5), a quarter of the terminal scales (0045 point 9).
- **`register.tsx`:** `desktopArt` and `sheetArt` pick the art for the band,
  pane and Collection tab:
  - a desktop sheet wins, drawn as an image (not `isInteractive`);
  - otherwise today's vector sprite;
  - otherwise text.

  The band scales it down to fit `maxRows × rowPx`. Sheet Svgs are cached per
  Modster, scale and look.
- **During a throw:** a Modster with a desktop sheet keeps its idle loop, and
  the dot count shows the throw. P5-47 draws desktop throws.
- **Shellsprout's desktop sheet:**
  - `plugin/content/modsters/sproutling/desktop.png`: 4 frames of 100×124,
    41 colours, 11.7 KiB, at 4 fps (base, sprout sway, base, blink).

    then the background was cleaned and the frames built in a scratch script.
  - @danielpg95 approved it, and it has a credits row.
  - Previews are in `docs/previews/shellsprout-desktop-p5-46*.`

## Problems and findings

- With pngjs's default compression the 4-frame sheet was 49.3 KiB, over the
  48 KiB budget. With `deflateLevel: 9` and `deflateStrategy: 1` it's
  11.7 KiB. The skills (P5-49, P5-50) should save desktop sheets that way.
- `tools/ref-sprite.mjs` leaves sky and ground from a busy background. The
  cleanup flood-filled sky and dirt from the outside only, recoloured
  enclosed sky specks as a leaf highlight, and cleared pale edge specks. This
  belongs in the contributor skill (P5-49).
