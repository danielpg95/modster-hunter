## Problems and findings

- Ghostty: `Image` renders at the right size (12 columns × 6 rows) but looks blurrier than the `Raster`. The terminal scales the bitmap with smoothing and the mod can't turn that off.
- iTerm: `Image` shows the dim `alt` text (`[blob]`) instead of pixels, as documented. Layout held.
- `Image` needs a graphics protocol (kitty, Ghostty), so it reaches few users and adds a second code path.
- `ImageProps` needs `alt` (required), `columns` and `rows` (1–255); `rgba` source is base64, at most 2 MiB decoded, 2048×2048 pixels.
- Sprites are small pixel art (decision 0001), so `Image` adds nothing the `Raster` doesn't draw crisply. It would only help hi-res art that doesn't fit the band.
