## Done

- **Planning** (PR #85, merged): decision [0026](../decisions/0026-biome-idle-scene.md),
  roadmap tasks P5-13 and P5-14, and the P5-13 claim.
- **Validator:** `background` takes 1–4 frames (`validateSprite`'s `singleFrame`
  became `maxFrames`). `biome.json` gains `backgroundFps` (1–12) and `particles`
  (`style`, `color`, `density`). Limits are in `CONTENT.biome` in `constants.ts`.
  Tests are in `plugin/tests/content/validate-biome.test.ts`,
  `validate-sprite.test.ts` and `load-content.test.ts`.
- **Compositor:** `plugin/hooks/render/idle-scene.ts` (pure).
  - `idleScene` resolves a biome's scene.
  - `makeParticles` draws 3 random numbers per particle.
  - `particlePixel` moves each style as a pure function of the tick.
  - `sceneCells` tiles the background and stamps particles, then calls `pixelsToCells`.
  - Tunables are in `SCENE` in `constants.ts`.
  - `sprite-cells.ts` now exports `spriteRgba` and `rgbaOf`.
  - Tests: `plugin/tests/render/idle-scene.test.ts`, with scripted random.
- **Band:** `bandView` has a `scene` kind when `sceneRows + 1 ≤ maxRows` on the
  terminal, and falls back to the idle line otherwise. Tested at `maxRows` 0, 1,
  2, 5, 7 and 15.
- **Register:** `register.tsx` draws a `Raster` keyed `scene` above the idle line.
  - `updateSceneTimer` runs the blit timer only while a turn or a subagent runs,
    with no encounter up and the band drawing the scene. It skips a tick while
    blits are in flight.
  - Held still, the scene shows frame 0 and particles stay where they stopped.
  - Particles are made once per band width.
- **Setting:** `showIdleScene` in `plugin.json` (default `true`).
- **Register tests:** `plugin/tests/register.test.tsx` with the fixture
  `plugin/tests/fixtures/scene-forest.ts`. They cover: the scene draws; it moves
  only during a turn; a subagent moves it; an encounter replaces and stops it, and
  it comes back after; the short-band fallback; the setting off; and a stop at
  session end. They count blits through a `ui.blit` test hook.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/CONTENT_FORMAT.md`: the biome example and table, and the `userConfig` table.
  - `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
  - The user skill's `reference/format.md` (field rows only).
- **Verified:** `pnpm typecheck` is clean and `claude plugin test ./plugin` passes
  514 tests (467 at start). `claude plugin validate ./plugin --strict` passes; the
  only new calls are `$.clock.every` and `$.ui.blit` via `updateSceneTimer`.
  `node --test tools/*.test.mjs` passes (23), and `node tools/check-content.mjs`
  passes.

## Problems and findings

- The worktree had no `plugin/.claude-plugin/types/` (generated, gitignored), so
  they were copied from the main checkout for `typecheck`.
- `ui.blit` can be hooked in tests (`on('ui.blit', …)` answering `{ value: {} }`)
  to count blits. No earlier test did this.
- Not tested: the scene timer stopping on module reload. It relies on the engine
  stopping `$.clock.every` timers on reload (DEVELOPMENT.md, "Timers").
