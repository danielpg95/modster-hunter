## Problems and findings

- **Reduction:**
  - `ref-sprite` keeps only the largest connected shape. Detached parts (music notes, dolls hanging on threads) need a copy that skips `keepLargest`, or need to be drawn by hand.
  - Thin parts (strings, threads, pins, needles) break up when shrunk. Strungking's strings were redrawn by hand.
- **Budget:** three or four fully different poses at 128 px often pass 48 KiB. Sharing still regions (outright, or where pixels differ only by reduction noise) brought them under. Lowering the colour count barely helped.
- **Locking parts of a pose:** pasting a fixed region from one pose into the others breaks continuity when the part around it moves (Ruffmoth's face, Knavelin's visor, Gigglet's lap doll). Use whole poses and share only below or beside the moving part.
- **Background bleed:** gaps enclosed by arms, threads and legs keep the background grey. Clear by colour distance to the reference's background; a plain "low saturation" test also eats pale highlights.
