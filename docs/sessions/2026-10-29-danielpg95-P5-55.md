## Decisions made

- Swinging parts get smooth in-between frames for every Modster (asked
  @danielpg95). Rotating a tail swung it open like a lid; bending it reads
  right.
- Musketail's cloak and tail stay still; only the sword, arm and feather move.
- Elderbark keeps its eyes closed in the idle, matching its dex entry.
- Pinekin and Hootlet were enlarged so every catcher fits (asked
  @danielpg95). The alternative, a decision letting the desktop throw grow to
  fit the catcher, was not taken.

## Problems and findings

- A throw plays only when the catcher fits inside the Modster's frame (0035
  point 8). On desktop both sheets are compared at their own sizes, so a small
  Modster sheet silently loses its throws. A 62×53 Pinekin missed 4 of 5
  catchers; this was only seen in the Code tab.
- Sizing desktop sheets by tier isn't in 0045 ("No per-tier sizes"), and it
  caused the problem above.
- Generated poses are separate drawings: use them for parts (eyes, a crystal),
  never as whole frames.

## Found along the way

  a built-in catcher. A check would have caught the Pinekin problem before the
  Code tab.

  can still miss small Modsters. Letting the desktop throw fit the catcher
  would need a decision amending 0035 point 8.
