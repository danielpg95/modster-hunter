## Problems and findings

- The black background of the reference stopped `ref-sprite`'s cut-out, and the reference had no dark outline of its own. What worked: flood the background to light grey from the corners, then paint a dark ring around the owl's mask. That ring also became the sprite's outline.
- A full-body front view with many small leaf feathers (the first two references) doesn't survive a 32 px shrink: leaves turn into noise. Tiled or stamped leaf patterns read as a grid, blobs, stripes or scales. A design with fewer, bigger shapes (one large wing, a big face) converts much better.
- Changing the target size moves which reference pixels land in each cell, so small details (a pupil) can lose the majority vote at one size and keep it at another.
