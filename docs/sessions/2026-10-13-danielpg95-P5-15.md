## Problems and findings

- The test engine makes `Math.random` read-only and has no seeded random. The
  miss test uses a 1% catch rate and asserts the wobble settles either way,
  checking the miss path when it happens (99 runs in 100). It can't fail by chance.
- Cause of the bug: `/clear` fires `session.end` (reason `clear`), whose hook
  calls `stopTimers()`, and no `session.start` follows.
