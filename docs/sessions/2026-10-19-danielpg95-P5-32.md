## Done

- `plugin/hooks/game/encounter-machine.ts`: a throw rolls whether it lands, then, for a miss only, the rock count: 1 or 2, from a second random number. - `plugin/hooks/register.tsx`:
  - `prefs:catcher` is read at session start.
  - After each machine step, `updateCatch` starts the animation when a new throw begins. It pins the catcher in use, prepares its art once (cached per catcher and Modster, cleared on reload), and blits `catchFrame` cells at 12 fps to every sprite site (band and pane), skipping a tick while blits are in flight. The animation runs until the throw ends, or for a catch until the `caught` beat ends inside the result card.
  - The Modster's idle loop waits during the throw, and its frame is put back afterwards.
  - The band and the pane draw `catchCells` while a throw plays, so a redraw shows the current frame.
  - With no catcher loaded, or one that doesn't fit, the throw keeps the idle loop and `wobble…`, as agreed.
