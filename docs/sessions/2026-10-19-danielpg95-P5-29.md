## Problems and findings

- **Which colors convert:** `npm run sprite` keeps only colors the art uses, so a `rimIndex` color has to appear in the tile. The net's knots double as the rim.
- **Brown flames:** the lantern's flame takes the Modster's color, which is dull on brown Modsters. The warm-white base keeps it reading as lit.
