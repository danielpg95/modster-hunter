## Problems and findings

- The `add-modster` desktop prompt's "Big eyes with a shine" line, together with cute wording ("plump", "rosy cheeks"), made the generator draw chibi Modsters. Prompts that ask for real creature proportions and add "Not chibi: no oversized head or eyes" fixed it.
- Poses whose tails reach under the next pose share columns. Cutting by connected shape, not by empty columns, separates them.
- A tall subject (Stalagmouse's icicle) or a wide one (Puddlefin, Smeltgolin) shrinks the creature, because every Modster is drawn at the same height. The icicle top was trimmed. Narrow or short frames were padded with transparent space to the 76×80 minimum.
- Stalagmouse's proud grin was lost in the reduction and was redrawn by hand.
- Puddlefin's reference kept the fish in place and moved the water instead, so its loop doesn't jump. Prismantis's reference ended on the strike instead of a salute.
- Not checked in the Code tab.
