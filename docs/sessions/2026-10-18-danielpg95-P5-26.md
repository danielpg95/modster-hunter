## Problems and findings

- `ref-sprite` failed on most references after Geodon:
  - it keeps only the largest shape, so a head separated by its outline
    (Geodon's first reference) was dropped;
  - it can't cut a painted checkerboard or a flat background that sits
    inside the outline;
  - shrinking by a non-integer factor turns patterns and small faces into
    noise.
  The scratchpad scripts that worked: cut a flat or checkerboard background by
  flooding low-saturation light pixels from the edges; find the reference's
  block size and offset from where colour edges line up, then sample one pixel
  per block; cap colours by merging the two closest colours until ≤ 24, which
  keeps rare hues like a gold beak (median cut lost them); restore outlines
  by marking any cell that is ≥ 30% outline in the reference.
- Gemini always saved 1408×768 JPEGs with blocks of about 23 px, whatever the
  prompt said about block counts. The creature's size in blocks only depends on
  how much of the frame it fills. Asking for "transparent" gave a painted
  checkerboard.
- Small features die in any shrink: eyes under 3 blocks, 1-block glints,
  scales under 4 blocks. Busy patterns over a coiled body read as a lump.
- When a reference is wider than 48 blocks, narrowing only the wings and
  keeping the body at its own pixel size beats shrinking everything.
