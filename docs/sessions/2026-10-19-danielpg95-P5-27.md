## Decisions made

- **Catchers, decision 0035 (with @danielpg95, 2026-10-19):**
  - The built-in catchers are the defaults, and players make their own with `create-modster-content`. The two-tone capsule was ruled out because it reads as a Pokéball.
  - A catcher isn't tied to a biome. The player picks one in a `5: Catchers` tab, saved as `prefs:catcher`, and the glass jar is the default.
  - Beats are declarative, with `contain` and `cover` as presets and `custom` for the player's own style.
  - One timeline for every catcher: 3 rocks for a catch, 1–2 for a miss, at most 2.4 s.
  - Text-only places show a dot count. Every catcher is available from the start, catchers only change looks, and they can't be turned off.
- **Format (P5-27):**
  - Frame 0 is the closed catcher, and the six beat names are fixed. `beats` lists only what differs from the preset; a `custom` catcher gives all six, each with its `modster`.
  - A catcher's sprite is at most 32×32 with up to 16 frames.
  - The optional `rimIndex` is added for the edge of a `stretch` shape. 0035's status line and the index say so.

## Problems and findings

- **Shared PNG cache:** the cache keyed files by Modster id, so a catcher could overwrite a Modster's cached sheet with the same id. Fixed with `<id>.catcher` keys; ids can't contain dots.
- **A test had to change:** an old test said `maxFrames` never raises the 8-frame limit. A catcher needs 16, so it now tests both limits.
