## Done

- `plugin/hooks/render/catch-timeline.ts`:
  - `throwingMs(outcome, rocks)` gives when the throwing phase ends: 2.4 s for a catch, 1.85 s or 2.35 s for a miss.
  - `catchBeatAt(ms, outcome, rocks)` gives the beat, the rock number and the progress through the beat. Past the end, it holds the last beat.
- `plugin/hooks/render/catch-beats.ts`: `resolveBeats(catcher)` fills in all six beats. The file's own fields win over the `contain` or `cover` preset; a `custom` catcher defaults to frames `[0]`, `over` and `arc`.
- `plugin/hooks/render/catch-frame.ts`: `prepareCatch` decodes the art once, paints `glowIndex` in the Modster's color, finds the Modster's outline and works out `fits`. `catchFrame(art, outcome, rocks, ms)` returns an RGBA `Frame` the size of the Modster's area.
  - Anchored catchers (`over` and `behind`):
    - The throw follows `arc` (with a spin when the frames are made), `drop` or `none`.
    - On `take`, the `lidRows` lid lifts; on `settle`, it drops back with a bump.
    - Each rock tilts frame 0 about its bottom center, leaning the other way on every other rock.
    - On `broken`, the lid flies off and the body disappears halfway through.
    - On `caught`, there's a white flash, then sparkles.
  - `stretch`: the frame is tiled inside a half-ellipse dome over the Modster's outline.
    - On `take`, the dome drops from the top.
    - On each rock, the tile shifts with the shake.
    - On `caught`, the dome shrinks with the Modster, and a knot appears when there's a rim.
    - On `broken`, it tears down the middle, and the halves spread and fall.
    - `rimIndex` draws the dome's edge and bottom row.
  - Modster actions: `visible`, `hidden`, `dim` (halfway to black), `shake`, `shrink-in` (toward the opening, flashing white), `shrink` (down to 0.65 where it stands), `hop-out` (3 px) and `pop-out`. The Modster keeps animating at its own fps.
  - Every pixel is opaque or empty, because terminal cells can't blend. Fades from the prototype became motion or a mix toward white.
- `CATCH` in `plugin/hooks/constants.ts` holds the beat lengths from 0035 point 5 (the `caught` beat is 0.7 s), plus the tilt, lid lift, shrink, hop, dim, margin, rim cutoff, sparkle colors and glow brightness.

## Decisions made

- **P5-28 split (asked by @danielpg95):** P5-28 is the frame function, P5-32 the timeline and blits, and P5-33 the dot count.
- **Tuning chosen while building:** the `caught` beat lasts 0.7 s (0035 left its length open; it plays in the result card). The tilt is 0.26 rad, the lid lifts 4 px, a cover catch shrinks to 0.65, and a hop is 3 px. All of these are in `CATCH`.
- **What `glowIndex` takes:** the Modster's most common color with summed RGB over 384, else its most common color. A cutoff of 240 picked mid-greens, so it was raised.
- **Draw order:** with `over`, the catcher draws over the Modster, except while it goes in or comes out (`shrink-in`, `pop-out`). With `behind`, the Modster draws on top. A `stretch` shape always draws over the Modster.
