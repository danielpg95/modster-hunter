## Problems and findings

- `$.store` has `keys()` and `delete()`; the test engine needs `store.keys` and
  `store.delete` handlers (added to the collection test harness and `memoryStore`).

  day instead would step the 250 ms encounter tick about 350k times.
- Two sessions folding in the same moment could still lose one fold. That
  isn't solvable without atomic writes; noted in 0029's reasoning.
