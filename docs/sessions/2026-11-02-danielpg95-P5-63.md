## Problems and findings

- `ref-sprite`'s default `--clear` tolerance (48) eats pale parts that are close to the light-grey background, such as Quartzclaw's pincer and Geodon's outline halo. `--tolerance 14`–`20` kept them. Background trapped between limbs (Geolem's arms) needs clearing by colour afterwards.
- `ref-sprite` keeps only the largest shape, so the generators' floating sparkles and sound rings are dropped. They were redrawn in the animation scripts.
- References come out at about 6–8 px per pixel. Reducing near that grid keeps outlines crisp; going much bigger makes edges stepped.
- Poses that the generator redrew differently (another face, a shifted body) can't be swapped in as whole frames. Align them first (Echobat's blink pose sat 3 px to the side), then take only the changed part.
- Colour blends between hues should go by hue, not RGB, or the in-between colours turn grey (Geolem).
- Geolem fills 64 colours exactly. The stone was reduced to 32 colours to make room for the six heart colour sets.
