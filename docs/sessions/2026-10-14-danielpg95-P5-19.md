## Problems and findings

- Shellsprout was redone at 28×32 (the new 32 px cap @danielpg95 set; a separate decision PR follows) from @danielpg95's own reference image. A straight shrink of a detailed image doesn't make a clean sprite: thick outlines turn into black blobs and faces blur. What worked: cut the background out along the image's dark outline, shrink by majority color per cell (keeps thin lines like the spiral), keep the shell from that, and repaint the sprout, head and face by hand.

- The automatic outline only paints onto empty pixels, so overlapping parts (an ear on a log, an arm on a cape) blend together. These need a manual outline against the other material; Hollowmask's ears and tail, and Musketail's arm, do this.
- At this size, faces fail fast on low contrast. What worked: flat face tones, eyes that contrast with what's around them (ice blue on a dark mask), and no ellipse shading near a muzzle (it reads as a beak).
