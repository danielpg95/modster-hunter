## Done

- **Validators:** a Modster or catcher needs a `sprite`, a `desktopSprite`, or
  both (`validate-modster.ts`, `validate-catcher.ts`).
- **Loaded types:** `LoadedModster.sprite` and `LoadedCatcher.sprite` are
  optional.
- **Loader:**
  - Content with desktop art only loads without a terminal sprite.
  - If its desktop sheet fails, it's skipped with an issue.
  - A named `sprite` that fails still skips the content, as before.
- **`register.tsx`:**
  - `DrawnModster`/`hasSprite` and `DrawnCatcher`/`hasCatcherSprite` narrow to
    content with terminal art, and every sprite read goes through them.
  - A desktop-only Modster draws as the compact text in the band, the pane and
    the Collection tab, on the terminal and on desktop. Its throw plays as the
    dot count.
  - The Catchers tab previews only Modsters and catchers with terminal art.
- **Catchers:** `catcherInUse` takes a `playable` check. A picked catcher with
  desktop art only plays as the default jar, and the Catchers tab marks the jar
  as in use.
- **Biomes:** a biome with `desktopBackground` only already showed the idle line
  alone. A test now covers it.
- **Tools:**
  - `check-content.mjs` skips the frame count and catcher fit checks for content
    with no terminal sprite.
  - The player helper's `preview-catcher` refuses content with no terminal
    sprite.
- **Docs:** `CONTENT_FORMAT.md` says `sprite` is optional with a
  `desktopSprite`, and the "Desktop art" section describes the fallbacks.
- **Tests:**

## Decisions made

- A `sprite` that's named but fails to load still skips the content, even with
  desktop art. Only a missing `sprite` field means desktop art only. This keeps
  terminal output unchanged for content that names a sprite.
- On desktop, desktop-only content also shows as text, or as the default
  catcher, until P5-46 to P5-48 draw desktop art.
