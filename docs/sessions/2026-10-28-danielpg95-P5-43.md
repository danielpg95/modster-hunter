## Decisions made

- 64 colours, each pixel opaque or clear.
- Backgrounds up to 48 px tall, 1–4 frames, 24 KiB.
- Catchers up to 96×96, 24 KiB. `glowColor` and `rimColor` replace palette
  indexes in a PNG, and `lidRows` counts desktop sheet rows.
- Desktop art shows at today's size: a quarter of the terminal scale. That's 1
  CSS px per pixel in the pane, 0.75 in the Collection, 0.5 in the band, and 2 in
  the idle scene.
- With both versions, they share the design but not the frame count or fps.

## Problems and findings

- The idle scene draws terminal backgrounds at `columnPx` (8) CSS px per pixel.
  So "same size as today" puts a 48 px desktop background at 2 CSS px per pixel,
  not 1.
