## Problems and findings

- Every reference came back 3:1 even when asked for 4:1; a 4:1 band was cropped from it.
- Dustwind's edges didn't meet (a lit dune face against a shaded slope), and no crop pair fixed it. Reducing 24 px wider than the tile and joining the overlap back in along a least-difference path per row (image quilting) made the seam smooth. Used for Saltbreeze too.
- Particle art at 8 fps over a bright scene: dark compact shapes read as insects, solid bright lines as lasers. Loose, low-contrast grains read as dust.
