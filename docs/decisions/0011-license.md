# 0011 — MIT for code, CC BY 4.0 for art

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Decided by:** @danielpg95 (accepted Claude's proposal)

## Context

An open repo needs a license before outside contributions arrive. Contributed
art also needs a clear license.

## Decision

- Code: MIT (`LICENSE` at the repo root).
- Built-in art and sounds: CC BY 4.0, credited per asset in
  `plugin/content/CREDITS.md`, unless the asset says otherwise.
- Contributors confirm in the PR template that they have the right to submit
  any art they add.
