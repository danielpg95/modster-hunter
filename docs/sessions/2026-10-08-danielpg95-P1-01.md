## Problems and findings

- `maxRows` seen (columns × rows): 140×40 → 15, 100×30 → 10, 80×24 → 7, 60×15 → 2. At 60×15 only 2 rows are free, so phase 2 needs a compact band mode.
- `e.props.isWorking` is true only while a model turn runs. A background shell command lets the turn end early and the band disappears with it.
- The hotkey fires after a short pause; the first `1` also lands in the prompt briefly and is removed when the button fires.
- A `$.state` contract must contain only `interface`/`type` declarations; a top-level `export {}` is refused.
- `$.clock.after` returns a `Timer` with `cancel()`; the callback closes over the `$` of the `turn.start` hook and `update($, atom, fn)` works from it.
- `gh` was missing; installed it. `GITHUB_TOKEN` in the environment authenticates it.
