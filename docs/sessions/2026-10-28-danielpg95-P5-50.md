## Done

- **Shared reduction:** `plugin/skills/create-modster-content/scripts/lib/ref-image.ts`
  holds the reduction, ported from `tools/ref-sprite.mjs` and typed: `cutout`,
  palette, shrink, `keepLargest`, `clearReaching`, `trimEdges`, `refImage`,
  `indexedRgba` and `parseRgb`. `tools/ref-sprite.mjs` imports it, so
  contributors and players reduce the same way. The tool keeps only the grid
  format and its `pngjs` file access.
- **Shared preview page:** `lib/desktop-preview.ts` builds it as a pure
  function, drawn with the mod's own `desktopSheetSvg` and `catchSvg` as
  images. It has no GIF and no `pngjs` or `omggif`. `tools/sprite.mjs
  --preview` uses it, and `tools/desktop-preview.mjs` is gone.
- **Helper commands** in `modster-content.mjs`, with no installs:
  - `reduce`: a reference becomes a base, with `--desktop`, `--clear`,
    `--trim-edges` and `--edges none`. It decodes with the mod's own PNG
    decoder and writes a level-9 PNG.
  - `check-desktop`: checks a sheet of any kind (modster, background,
    catcher, particles) and writes the preview page with `--preview`.
    Catchers throw at Shellsprout and Mossmaul.
- **Skill:** `create-modster-content` has a "Desktop, terminal or both"
  section: what each means, what to recommend to whom, and what the other
  surface sees. It adds a desktop path for:
  - Modsters, from a reference with `reduce --desktop`;
  - biome backgrounds (crop between trunks, sway only low things);
  - particles;
  - catchers.

  `reference/format.md` gains a "Desktop art" table. "Without Node" explains
  what desktop art can and can't do without the helper.
