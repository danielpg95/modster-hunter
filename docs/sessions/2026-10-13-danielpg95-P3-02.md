## Decisions made

- **List plus detail**, not a grid of sprite tiles. @danielpg95 proposed it:
  sprites (up to 33×16 cells) don't fit tiles, and inline panes have about 8 rows.
- **`j`/`k` move the selection.** The arrows are the engine's scrolling.
- Kept suggestions 3–7: dex order, a `Caught n/m` line, a still gray
  silhouette, the selection kept across tabs, and records of unloaded Modsters
  left out. Dropped suggestion 8 (a 200-Modster test); scrolling is tested with
  31.
- **One layout per pane, whatever is selected** (after the first hand check).
- Narrow docks stack the detail under the list instead of swapping to a detail
  view with a back key, so no back key was needed.
- "Biomes found in" is the habitat (biomes that list it, 0017 point 5), not
  the biomes it was caught in.

## Problems and findings

- The test engine has no `$.ui.close`, so a pane's close can't be tested. It
  was checked by hand.
- A hook can't be added with `on(...)` after a test first calls `$`. To change
  the store mid-test, keep the stub's backing object and write to it.
- With the fixed box sized to the 33×16 built-ins, inline panes never show the
  sprite. A docked pane shows it beside the text from about 97 columns, or
  above it with about 25 rows. @danielpg95 approved that trade.
